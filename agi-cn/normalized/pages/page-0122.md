    Expanding on this, the system’s operation is not a single query-response
event but a managed, long-running process. It begins by deconstructing a
user’s prompt into a multi-point research plan (see Fig. 6.1), which is then
presented to the user for review and modification. This allows for a collabora-
tive shaping of the research trajectory before execution. Once the plan is
approved, the agentic pipeline initiates its iterative search-and-analysis loop.
This involves more than just executing a series of predefined searches; the
agent dynamically formulates and refines its queries based on the information
it gathers, actively identifying knowledge gaps, corroborating data points, and
resolving discrepancies.
