Key Takeaways
Key takeaways include:

• Goal Setting and Monitoring equips agents with purpose and mechanisms
  to track progress.
• Goals should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-­
  bound (SMART).
• Clearly defining metrics and success criteria is essential for effective
  monitoring.
• Monitoring involves observing agent actions, environmental states, and
  tool outputs.
• Feedback loops from monitoring allow agents to adapt, revise plans, or
  escalate issues.
