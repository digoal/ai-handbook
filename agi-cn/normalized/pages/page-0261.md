This system uses a resource-aware optimization strategy to handle user queries
efficiently. It first classifies each query into one of three categories to deter-
mine the most appropriate and cost-effective processing pathway. This
approach avoids wasting computational resources on simple requests while
ensuring complex queries get the necessary attention. The three categories are:

• simple: For straightforward questions that can be answered directly with-
  out complex reasoning or external data.
• reasoning: For queries that require logical deduction or multi-step thought
  processes, which are routed to more powerful models.
• internet_search: For questions needing current information, which auto-
  matically triggers a Google Search to provide an up-to-date answer.
