Conclusion
In conclusion, the Planning pattern is a foundational component that elevates
agentic systems from simple reactive responders to strategic, goal-oriented
executors. Modern large language models provide the core capability for this,
autonomously decomposing high-level objectives into coherent, actionable
steps. This pattern scales from straightforward, sequential task execution, as
demonstrated by the CrewAI agent creating and following a writing plan, to
more complex and dynamic systems. The Google DeepResearch agent exem-
plifies this advanced application, creating iterative research plans that adapt
and evolve based on continuous information gathering. Ultimately, planning
provides the essential bridge between human intent and automated execution
for complex problems. By structuring a problem-solving approach, this pat-
tern enables agents to manage intricate workflows and deliver comprehensive,
synthesized results.
