   CoD (Chain of Debates) is a formal AI framework proposed by Microsoft
where multiple, diverse models collaborate and argue to solve a problem,
moving beyond a single AI’s “chain of thought.” This system operates like an
AI council meeting, where different models present initial ideas, critique each
other’s reasoning, and exchange counterarguments. The primary goal is to
enhance accuracy, reduce bias, and improve the overall quality of the final
answer by leveraging collective intelligence. Functioning as an AI version of
peer review, this method creates a transparent and trustworthy record of the
reasoning process. Ultimately, it represents a shift from a solitary Agent pro-
viding an answer to a collaborative team of Agents working together to find a
more robust and validated solution.

ines discussion as a dynamic, non-linear network rather than a simple chain.

relationships like ‘supports’ or ‘refutes,’ reflecting the multi-threaded nature
of real debate. This structure allows new lines of inquiry to dynamically
